THE CURRENT DIRECTION
Symmetrical dog ears, a transparent diamond nose and an outlined wordmark with a
diamond i-dot. Ink and warm paper carry the identity; acid and lilac add character.
The editorial mascot uses the founder's dog's proportions in black ink, with a
narrow acid collar and a single lilac diamond tag. No bow tie.

QUICK RULES
Use the supplied logo paths. Do not retype, stretch, rotate, outline or add effects.
Keep one nose-diamond width of clear space outside the visible logo bounds.
Use ink on paper, paper on ink, or acid on ink. Bright accents need ink text.
Use the micro mark for the smallest icon sizes; full-size details will disappear.
Pair state colors with a visible label and distinctive symbol. Unknown is neutral.
Claimed, observed, accepted and unknown are evidence labels, not a progress ladder.
The primary line stays primary. Good work. Clear boundaries. is secondary.
Use access, grant, authority, and what it may do for the delegation model.

STATUS AND RELEASE
This is a first-edition brand direction, not a claim that the software has shipped.
The design is written down and a first experiment is under way. The intended
environment is a Linux layer with a headless runtime and inspector.
The fixed evaluation inspector screens have not been changed; outcome tokens
are guidance for later styling. The landing page is a static concept, not a site.
The repository, design documents and license headers retain OrcaOS pending the
founder's name-check gate. This kit has not published or renamed anything.

LICENSING AND PRODUCTION
The founder brief specifies GPLv3 runtime and MIT SDK, interfaces and examples.
Those software licenses do not automatically license the name or logo. No new
brand-asset license or trademark policy is set by this kit. The founder should
choose the usage policy and holder before inviting public redistribution.
Font licenses are included beside the font files. Preserve them when redistributing.
SVG and PNG masters are RGB. For print, use true-black one-ink logo files or
obtain a CMYK/spot-color proof from the printer. No untested Pantone match is claimed.
The mascot is 1536x1024 pixels: about 5.12x3.41 inches at 300ppi. Use a suitably
sized reproduction; request a larger master for large print work.
Sticker and poster files are artwork masters, not vendor-specific dielines.

PROVENANCE
An AI creative team developed original work using public process references and
the founder's brief and feedback. Allan Peters and Wharton did not participate
or endorse the work. The mascot reference photo remains private and is excluded.
Original character and mark studies are kept outside the main kit so they cannot
be mistaken for the selected production identity.
