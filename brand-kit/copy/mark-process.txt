CHOICE / THE SYMMETRICAL COMPANION
A frontal dog with symmetrical rounded ears and mirrored angled folds. Calm circular eyes and an open diamond cut form the face. The tilted geometry gives personality, while the continuous silhouette remains easy to reproduce in one ink. The diamond is structural negative space, not a gem illustration or badge.

Rationale: front-facing attention + approachable companion. The diamond cut is a mnemonic of the name and a repeatable geometric device. It is not a visual security guarantee. Symmetry gives the dog a calm, deliberate presence; the ear folds preserve an unmistakable canine silhouette. The original wordmark uses Space Grotesk Bold with optical spacing and a custom diamond i-dot. The diamond recurs exactly once in the wordmark so the reference stays restrained.

ROUTES AND REVISION
Original profile studies were rejected after strategic research flagged the existing Diamond Dogs military-emblem context. Three frontal routes were compared: a softer retriever, an asymmetric folded ear, and a more faceted head. The middle route was initially selected by the creative lead. After the user requested symmetry and diamond-ear exploration, the team compared the original, symmetrical angled folds, and symmetrical small diamond folds. The founder explicitly selected option 02, the symmetrical angled-fold route, as the first-edition production choice. All three studies are retained. Avoid presenting rejected studies as alternate official logos.

PRODUCTION FILES
logos/ contains 5 lockup families in 5 colorways, each in SVG and PNG:
- mark: full-detail symbol
- micro: ear cuts removed and eyes enlarged for small uses
- wordmark: all text outlined; diamond i-dot
- horizontal: preferred signature
- stacked: centered compact signature
Colorways: ink #17191B, true black #000000, paper #F4F0E8, true white #FFFFFF, acid #DFFF00.
SVG files have transparent grounds, no external fonts, no filters, no strokes. Internal cutouts are genuinely transparent via evenodd fill. All silhouettes use editable paths. PNG files retain alpha. No SVG font dependency.

USAGE
Primary signature: ink on paper or paper on ink. Acid only on ink and with sufficient visual contrast. Do not use acid on white/paper. Use full symbol from 32px canvas up; micro at 16–31px canvas. Preferred app/avatar presentation at least 48px. Favicon-only 16px has limited detail by necessity. Horizontal lockup: minimum 240px digital width; standalone wordmark: minimum 140px. In print, test at actual output size; recommended horizontal width >=40mm, mark canvas >=8mm.
Clear space: at least one diamond-nose width (48 grid units) on every side of the visible silhouette or wordmark. No other elements may enter this space. Asset canvas includes some padding but not the full clear space; add it in layout.
Do not stretch, rotate the mark, outline it, close the nose, add a badge/shield/diamond surround, change ear proportions, add gradients to the mark, use glow or shadows, or replace the wordmark with ordinary typed text. Do not use the full detail icon at favicon size; choose micro.
