Mascot comparison requested by founder
Disney / Lady and the Tramp official film page
https://movies.disney.com/lady-and-the-tramp
No Disney imagery was supplied to the image-generation tool. The final character
uses the founder's personal dog photograph for anatomy and the original ink
illustration for visual treatment. The private photograph is not distributed.

Image-generation method
Built-in image_gen. Final generation prompt: illustration/prompt-personal.txt.
The mascot is an editorial illustration; the mark and wordmark are vector artwork.

Accessibility
Contrast ratios were calculated from the actual sRGB token values. The report
records all pairs and thresholds. Contrast checks do not establish complete
WCAG conformance for a future application; keyboard, semantics, focus behavior
and assistive-technology use must be assessed in the implemented interface.
