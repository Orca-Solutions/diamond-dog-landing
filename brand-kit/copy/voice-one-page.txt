DIAMOND DOG / VOICE IN ONE PAGE

Delegate work, not your identity.
a delegation-first operating environment

HONEST. PLAIN-SPOKEN. WRY. CALM. ON YOUR SIDE.
Write for capable people who already automate and hesitate to give software the keys to everything. Developers are a first audience alongside them. Recognise the hesitation without frightening anyone.

SAY WHAT IS TRUE TODAY
The design is written down. A first experiment is under way. Nothing has shipped. The intended environment runs on Linux. Apps, scripts and AI participants follow the same rules. Describe the design and the experiment; distinguish them from demonstrated results.

MAKE THE ARRANGEMENT CONCRETE
Say what a job may read, what it may change, and what needs the person's sign-off. Prefer a specific folder, message or action to a broad technical promise. Use access, grant, authority, bounded activity and participant. Explain unfamiliar terms once.

LET THE RECORD BE PRECISE
Keep claimed, observed, accepted and unknown distinct. Identify what the statement is based on. Unknown is an honest outcome, not a cheerful checkmark or a failure by default.
“ The mail service accepted the message at 14:02. Delivery to the inbox was not observed. ”
“ We don't know yet. Here's what we saw. ”
Never imply that cancelling reverses an effect already sent to an outside service.

PUT THE WINK IN THE RIGHT PLACE
Website, README, stickers, docs asides and empty states can play. Aim the joke at overbroad access and unhelpful conventions. Never at a user or a neighbouring project. Approval, unknown outcomes, payments and personal records get sober, exact language.
“ A little less blind faith. ”
“ Quite particular about who gets to do what. ”
“ Fetches what you sent it for. ”
One wink at a time. The primary line remains the primary line.

KEEP THE CHARACTER OURS
A little artful independence; no lyrics, celebrity likenesses, signature makeup or album imagery. A working helper with a dry sense of humour. No threat theatre. No special status for AI. No claims the experiment has not earned.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
Check the actual state and scope. Keep the repository's OrcaOS name until the founder's name-check gate clears. Leave evaluation inspector screens as the protocol specifies. GPLv3 runtime; MIT SDK, interfaces and examples. The name and logo require a separate trademark policy.
