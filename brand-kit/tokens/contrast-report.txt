USAGE
Use Ink text on Acid, Lilac and Ember. Do not use these bright accents as body text on Paper.
State colour is always paired with a visible label and distinctive symbol. Symbols are decorative when the adjacent text label conveys the same meaning.
Claimed: participant report. Observed: evidence seen. Accepted: named service acknowledged submission. Unknown: available evidence does not establish outcome. Error is separate.
These evidence labels are not a progress ladder. An observed acceptance can coexist with unknown delivery. Unknown is neither success nor error.
State tokens are a future styling proposal. Do not apply them to inspector screens fixed for the upcoming evaluation.
A focus ring needs visible separation: recommended 3px ring with 3px offset against the theme surface. Validate any different adjacent colour.
Motion is decorative only: 140ms for small changes, 220ms for transitions. Reduced motion disables both. No result is implied by animation.
